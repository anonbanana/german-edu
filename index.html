02 — About us

About Us

German Plus Consultant is a consultancy for the German language, for bachelor's and master's studies in Germany, for vocational education (Ausbildung) and for careers in Germany. German Plus was founded in 2008 as an officially registered consulting and service company with the Ministry of Education and Culture of the Republic of Indonesia.

We have long experience and a trusted reputation with everyone who wants to learn German, continue with a degree in Germany, take part in an Ausbildung program or build a career in Germany. Our services cover every step — from preparing for German courses and securing a place at university, to helping you find an Ausbildung placement and handling all documents, including visas, until you are ready to leave for Germany.

03 — Services

German Language Courses

Start your preparation with a German course!

German is an essential part of preparing for studies, Ausbildung or a career in Germany. We provide language courses for a variety of purposes:

German Courses A1 to B2

A1 – B2

German Plus offers German courses from level A1 to B2 — online or on site, in groups or private. To keep learning outcomes as strong as possible, we limit group classes to a maximum of three participants.

Teaching is led by professional instructors with many years of experience in Germany. The material follows the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR / GER) and is adapted to each participant's needs and goals.

B1 & B2 Exam Preparation — Goethe-Institut / telc / ÖSD

B1 – B2

Focused preparation for the B1 and B2 German examinations held by the Goethe-Institut, telc or ÖSD. In this course you receive material and practice questions that match the format and requirements of the exam exactly.

Studienkolleg Entrance Exam Preparation (Aufnahmeprüfung)

Studienkolleg

A dedicated program to prepare you for the language entrance examination (Aufnahmeprüfung) at a Studienkolleg in Germany — with material and practice questions tailored to the exam.

04 — Services

Studienkolleg & University Studies (B.Sc./M.Sc.)

A guaranteed place at a partner Studienkolleg!

Studienkolleg

The Studienkolleg is a one-year academic preparation program in Germany for international prospective students, taken before starting a bachelor's degree at a German university.

The Studienkolleg offers several types of preparatory classes, aligned with the subject area you plan to study.

After completing the Studienkolleg and passing the final examination (Feststellungsprüfung), you are qualified to begin a bachelor's degree in Germany.

German Plus guarantees a place at a partner Studienkolleg, helps you obtain a university acceptance letter for bachelor's or master's studies, finds accommodation and handles all other documents required for your studies — including visa preparation — until you are ready to leave for Germany.

05 — Services

Ausbildung Support

Study and work in Germany!

Ausbildung

Ausbildung is Germany's dual vocational training system: it combines theoretical learning at a vocational school (Berufsschule) with practical training in a company.

Participants gain the knowledge and skills of their occupation while collecting real work experience at the same time. An Ausbildung usually lasts two to three and a half years depending on the profession, and can be the entry point to a professional career in Germany.

Our service

We help you find an Ausbildung place based on current vacancies and company needs in Germany, assist with accommodation and handle all other documents required for your training — including visa preparation. We strive to match you with placements that fit your profile and qualifications. The admission decision always rests with the company in Germany.

Working as a Skilled Professional (Fachkraft)

Start your path to a career in Germany!

Germany opens up career opportunities for professional workers (Fachkraft) whose qualifications and skills match the labour market. If you have a relevant academic background or work experience, working in Germany can be a chance to develop your career and gain international experience. The process, however, requires the right preparation — from your German language skills to meeting employment requirements and preparing the necessary documents.

Our service

We help you find job opportunities that match current vacancies and company needs in Germany, assist with accommodation and handle all other documents required for working in Germany — including visa preparation. We strive to find roles that fit your profile and qualifications. Hiring decisions are made through the company's recruitment process and its needs in Germany.
